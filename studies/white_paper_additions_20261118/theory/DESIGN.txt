T12 C identification and uncertainty

Each independent query has M~Bernoulli(rho). Conditional on M, channel flags
are independent. Unmarked TPRs are f=0.01. Marked sensitivities are
s1=f+(1-f)*theta and s2=f+(1-f)*theta^2. Thus marginal detector flags are
correlated because they share M. The joint four-category multinomial is
sampled exactly, avoiding the need to allocate n synthetic texts in memory.
One query means one output checked by both channels, hence two detector calls.

Writing a=(q1-f)/(1-f)=rho*theta and b=(q2-f)/(1-f)=rho*theta^2,
the interior inverse is theta=b/a and rho=a^2/b. The Jacobian determinant
is (1-f)^2*rho*theta^2, so identification fails at theta=0 and precision
deteriorates near the singular boundary. At rho=0 theta is unidentified.
Different sensitivities alone are not sufficient in general; their response
curves must yield a full rank mapping, as these chosen curves do inside.
One channel at q1=0.2575 only gives rho*theta=0.25 and rho in [0.25,1].

Each marginal probability gets a 97.5 percent exact Clopper Pearson
interval. The union bound gives joint coverage at least 95 percent without
assuming independence across channels. Transform to a rectangle with
a in [A,B], b in [C,D], intersecting the model's wedge a^2<=b<=a.
For l=max(A,C,0) and h=min(B,sqrt(D),1), a nonempty regular feasible
set projects onto rho from l^2/min(D,l) to h^2/max(C,h^2).
If the rectangle contains the origin, rho ranges over [0,1].
An empty set is flagged and conservatively replaced by [0,1].
The interval midpoint is used for the within two percentage point claim.

For each rho and theta, all frozen query-grid candidates receive 2000
selection replicates. The smallest with at least 97.5 percent narrow
intervals is selected. Exactly one independent batch of 10000 confirmation
replicates then estimates coverage and the probability of width<=0.04.
Success requires the lower bound of that probability's two-sided exact
95 percent Monte Carlo interval to be at least 0.95. No confirmation-guided
search or retry is permitted. If no candidate passes selection, the maximum
candidate is confirmed for a descriptive cap result, without a precision claim.
This is conservative conditional evidence, not calibration of actual short
text watermark detectors. Near theta=0 no finite uniform sample guarantee
is possible. Query-grid limits and all failed cells remain reported.

The earlier audit used one detector with separately calibrated sensitivity
in two strata. It did not jointly identify marking fraction and strength
or determine this two channel precision query count.
